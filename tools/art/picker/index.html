Pick your art

Click Use on site on every piece you want on the wall. Click Hero on the painting you'd want at the top of the site (one or a few, and I'll rotate them). Title, year and medium are optional but help. Each pick gets straightened, cropped and colour-corrected automatically; if a crop looks wrong, hit Fix crop and drag the corners. Where you took more than one photo of a piece, the small squares let you choose the best photo. Everything saves automatically.

Fix the crop

Drag the four green dots onto the corners of the canvas or page, then hit Apply. The cleaned-up version shows below.
